DESKTOP APP FOR NEXTCLOUD PASSWORDS

Nextcloud Passwords
on your Windows desktop.

NcPasswords is a Windows app for Passwords, the password manager you can install on your Nextcloud. Sign in once, and your saved logins are always a click away: search for an entry, copy its password, and get on with your day, without opening Nextcloud in the browser.

SECURITY

Your data stays on your PC, encrypted.

Encrypted on disk

Your Nextcloud login and the local copy of your passwords are encrypted with Windows' built-in protection (DPAPI), so only your Windows account on this PC can read them. For extra safety, set a local password when you sign in: NcPasswords then asks for it at every start, and other programs running under your account can't read the data without it.

Works offline

NcPasswords keeps an encrypted copy of your passwords, so you can still look things up without an internet connection. The copy is updated every time you open the app.

Connects only to your server

No analytics, no tracking, no servers of ours in between. The app only talks to the Nextcloud address you enter.

FEATURES

Simple and quick to use.

NcPasswords does one job: getting you to the right password quickly. It shows the passwords you keep in Nextcloud; adding and editing them still happens in Nextcloud itself.

  • Folders and entries in one list Your Nextcloud folders and entries appear together in a single tree, much like in Password Safe. Open the folders you need and ignore the rest.
  • A tidy list Each entry shows just its name and username. The address, notes, tags and custom fields are in the details view.
  • Copy in one step Double-click an entry to copy its password, or press Ctrl+C to copy the username. The clipboard is cleared again after 30 seconds.
  • Search as you type Type a few letters to filter by name, username, address or notes. Only folders with matching entries stay visible.
  • Waits in the tray Closing the window keeps NcPasswords running in the system tray, ready for the next password. Choose Exit from the tray icon to close it completely.

GET STARTED

Set up in a minute.

01

Download

Get the installer below. No account or registration needed.

02

Install

It installs for your Windows user only, so you don't need administrator rights.

03

Sign in

Enter your Nextcloud address, username and password. If you use two-factor authentication, create an app password in Nextcloud and use that instead. Accounts with client-side encryption aren't supported.
